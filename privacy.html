Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 10, 2026

Multi Health Consulting ("we", "us") operates this website to help Florida consumers explore health insurance options. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Information you submit in our forms: name, phone number, email address, ZIP code, household size, estimated income range, who needs coverage, and preferred language.
  • Consent records: the consent statement you agreed to, the date and time, the page URL, and your browser information.
  • Basic technical data such as device and browser type.

How we use it

  • To have a licensed insurance agent contact you about health insurance options, by phone, text message, or email, as you consented on the form.
  • To help you compare, apply for, and manage health insurance coverage if you choose to work with us.
  • To keep records required by law and by the Health Insurance Marketplace, including records of your consent.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell your information to third parties.
  • We do not share your information with anyone other than Multi Health Consulting's licensed agents and staff, the insurance companies and the Health Insurance Marketplace when you choose to apply for coverage, and service providers that help us operate this website.

Text messages

If you consented to text messages, you can opt out at any time by replying STOP. Message and data rates may apply. Consent to receive calls or texts is not a condition of purchasing anything.

Your choices

You may ask us to correct or delete the information you submitted, or to stop contacting you, at any time by emailing us or telling any of our agents. Some records (such as Marketplace consent records) must be retained as required by law even after you opt out of contact.
