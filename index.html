Common questions

Is this really free?

Yes. Licensed agents are compensated by insurance companies, so our help costs you nothing — and your monthly premium is the same whether you use an agent or not.

Do I qualify for a $0/month plan?

It depends on your household size, income, and other factors. Many Florida families qualify for large subsidies that can bring premiums to $0/month — the only way to know is to check. Not everyone qualifies.

Can I enroll right now, or do I have to wait?

Outside the annual Open Enrollment Period, you can still enroll if you've had a qualifying life event — like losing other coverage, moving, getting married, or having a baby. We'll check for free.

What information do I need?

To see your options: just your ZIP code, household size, and estimated income. To enroll: basic info like dates of birth and, for some, immigration documents. Your agent walks you through everything.
